# Karting apparel

Kart suits are **abrasion**, not fire. No harness/suspension → you get thrown around and may slide on asphalt.

## Suit

GCR: valid **CIK-FIA homologation on the collar** = MSA-approved for karting. One-piece, **two fabric layers**, outer Cordura or Nylon, cotton-like inner. Sporting regs: CIK number or MSA approval.

- **Level 1:** usually Nylon outer
- **Level 2:** usually Cordura — higher abrasion, stretch, wicks sweat

Covers neck, close at wrists/ankles. Fit **in a seated kart posture**. Buy branded (Alpinestars, OMP, Puma, Sparco) from a proper outlet — fake CIK patches exist. Importing “clothing” into SA can attract ~**40%** duty.

## Boots

Must cover and protect the **ankles**. Thin flexible soles for pedal feel. Fit with race socks. Leather or suede; laces and/or Velcro. Heel rubber for floor-pan wear. Torn boots or no socks = no race.

## Neck brace

Kart helmets have no FHR posts. Options from foam collar to Leatt. Sits between shoulders and helmet; limits flexion/extension and axial load. Recommended especially for juniors.

## Gloves

MSA: cover the hands. CIK: cover hands **and wrists**, or FIA 8877-2022. Snug fingertips, no bunching in the palm, padding on knuckles/backs.

## Rib / chest

Common injury from seat, wheel or another kart. Protectors spread load and resist penetration. Europe is moving toward mandatory breast-plate style.

Elbow/knee pads optional extras. Do not cheap out on safety.
