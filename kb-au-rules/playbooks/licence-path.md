# Australian licence path

1. Find a club → join in **KOMP** → apply for the matching **Cadet 9 / Cadet 12 / Junior / Senior** licence.
2. Safety training. If D or 8-Day: **ODS** then **P plate**.
3. Under 18: Participant’s Licence on the adult.
4. Race **4** meetings (endorsed) → apply for **C**. National Cadet/KA3 needs C.
5. **6** more at C, or a listed National top-5 → **B**. Needed for KA2, Junior Max, TaG 125, X30, Rotax 125, DD2, Rok GP, Open Performance.
6. State Championship top-3 → **A**.
7. VIC/SA juniors/cadets: **2× D→C endorsements** before State Series / Cup / Trophy / Zonal.
8. TaG Restricted at State Championship: usually **max C** (40+ may keep A/B). Winning Restricted Light/Medium/Heavy at a State Championship bars Restricted at the next year’s State Championship.
9. Recreational only: **E Grade**. Try-one-meeting: **8-Day** (once per year). Vintage: Vintage licence. Officials/practice control: KAC.
