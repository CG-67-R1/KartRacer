# SA 2026 State Regulations

Final V2, 3 February 2026. Overrides: `data/state-overrides.json` → `sa`.

## General

- **4SS Senior Heavy = 175 kg** (national 170).
- Cadet 9 + 4SS Cadet + Cadet 12 consolidation **not recommended**; if done, Cadet 9 / 4SS Cadet at the **rear**, ~**25 m** gap at the start.
- State Series/Cup/Trophy/Zonal: Cadet 9, KA3, TaG Restricted engines **sealed**.
- **Cameras compulsory** from first practice at Zonal / State Series / Cup / Trophy.
- Cadet/Junior **D Grade:** 2× D→C endorsements before Zonal / State Series / Cup / Trophy.
- Full Course Yellow **may** be used at State Series and similar.
- SKA within 48 h of those events: entered drivers, entered classes, race-order sessions.

## Festival State Cup (State Series)

Must offer: Cadet 12; KA3 Junior L/H; KA3 Senior Light; 4SS Senior M/H (Club status for 4SS in FSC); TaG Restricted L/M; TaG 125 Championship; TaG 125 Masters.

Entry **$125**. Cadet/Junior: no minimum field. Series awards: min 5 entries at ≥ 3 rounds. Drop **five** lowest results. Min 3 rounds to be eligible. 4SS in FSC is treated as Club competition status under the national standing-reg note.
