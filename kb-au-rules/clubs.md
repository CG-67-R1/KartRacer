# Australian kart clubs by state

**Structure:** Sport Aus / FIA → **Karting Australia** (NSO) → **member state associations** → **affiliated clubs** → KOMP licences. See the [KA corporate-structure PDF](https://kartingaustralia.mymedia.delivery/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/KA-Corporate-Structure-2023.pdf).

The [national Club Finder](https://www.karting.net.au/how-to-get-started-in-karting/club-finder/) is a map onto those affiliated clubs. Join the club in **KOMP**, then apply for the licence.

**Karting NSW** (`kartingnsw.com.au`) is a **separate** association (not a KA member). Its clubs use a KNSW licence, not a KA licence. Listed last so you do not mix portals.

**Events:** KA club meetings are entered on the KOMP calendar. Each state also publishes a calendar. Club sites/Facebook often list practice days.

National phone: **1300 30 5278**. Machine table: `data/clubs.json`.
