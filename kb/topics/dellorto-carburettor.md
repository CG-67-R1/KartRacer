# The Dellorto carburettor

Jobs: atomise fuel to a stable A/F, and meter mixture with the slide (or butterfly). Pump petrol ignites ~**7:1 to 20:1**; stoich **14.7:1 by mass** (~9000 L air per 1 L fuel). SA Rok OKJ uses Dellorto VHSH 30 (page also mentions VHST 24 BS — confirm current spec).

Venturi vacuum draws emulsified fuel from the bowl via main jet + emulsion tube. Float + needle valve hold bowl level. Heavier float / higher arm = richer. Float **mass** is regulated; arm height has a small tolerance.

## Circuits

Idle to ~1/8 throttle (richer) → progression to ~1/2 → main to WOT. Idle/progression jets usually **fixed**. Legal tuners: **main jet** and **needle clip**.

Needles: 5 grooves. Clip in **groove 1 (top) = leaner**; **5 = richer**. Class needles only (200+ Dellorto shapes exist).

**Mixture screw:** Dellorto — **upstream of slide = air** (in = richer); **downstream = fuel** (in = leaner). Air screw = fine point; fuel screw = stubby. Idle with the throttle screw. Start **1.5 turns out** where given.

Codes: number = bore mm (14-12 = 14→12 venturi). S/P/V = slide / piston+needle / flat valve+needle. H = horizontal. S/D after size = screws Sinistra/Destra. L = clamp, B = rubber spigot, X = Rotax.

**Rotax float:** invert, bowl gasket off, gauge **277400** — both arms in the slot. PHBG ideal arm **16 ± 0.5 mm** inverted.

Carb is **unsealed** — competitor owns exclusions. Best power is richer than 14.7 (see lambda in temps/jetting topics).
