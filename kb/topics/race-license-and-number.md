# Race licence and number

Cape Town Rok/Rotax sits under **MSA**. You need a licence, a number, and the current rules. Join WPMC and pay pit/practice fees first.

## Licence

- Club/regional championship only → **Regional** licence
- Rok or Rotax Nationals → **National** (one-event National upgrades exist)
- Apply at [msaonline.co.za](https://www.msaonline.co.za) using the **competitor’s** ID. Pay, download the licence
- Flag/safety test is required before MSA issues the kart licence (see intro-for-rookies)

## Number

- **Rok:** number form (Excel/PDF), give **three** preferred numbers, send with licence copy to the WC MSA office
- **Rotax** (regional or National): register the number through the Rotax channel linked from the source page

## Rules

Ignorance is not a defence. Download current WPMC + National Rok/Rotax sporting and technical regs and keep them updated.
