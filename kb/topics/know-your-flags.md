# Know your flags

Use current **ASN / event SRs** for the official flag table — you sit a flag test before the licence is issued.

- Marshals cannot shout over engine noise. Flags are the only warning.
- **Yellow:** danger / kart or marshal near the circuit. Slow, be ready to stop, **no overtaking** until you leave the yellow zone. Passing under yellow = **exclusion**.
- **Chequered:** race over. Slow, gap to the kart ahead (nosecone), proceed to scales.
- **Black and orange:** typically mechanical / equipment issue — unsecured helmet or neck-brace straps can earn this flag until fixed.
- **False start:** multiple flags; hands up; reform as a formation lap.
- Not knowing a flag is **not** a defence.

See `must-know-driving-rules` and the licence flag syllabus for the full set (green, red, blue, black, white, etc.).
