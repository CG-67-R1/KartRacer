# VIC 2026 State Regulations

Final V4, 17 February 2026. Overrides: `data/state-overrides.json` → `vic`.

## General

- Cadet / Junior / TaG Restricted at State Series: engines **sealed**; restrictors may be checked.
- Reverse grid if **≤ 8** karts — named in Supplementary Regulations.
- Cadet/Junior **D Grade:** 2× D→C endorsements before Zonal / State Series / Cup / Trophy.
- SKA within 48 h of those events: approval + entered drivers only.
- Trackside assistants may be mandatory (roster; refusal → two-place grid penalty next non-final section).

## Victorian Country Series

Must: Cadet 9, Cadet 12, KA3 Junior L/H, KA3 Senior L/M, TaG 125 L/H. Optional: Victorian Combined Masters, TaG Restricted L/M/H. Min **6** entries at ≥ 5 rounds for series awards. Entry **$125**. Drop lowest round (DSQ cannot be dropped).

## Golden Power Series

Same must-list; optional adds **KA2**. Min **8** to constitute. Licence: Karting Victoria, KANSW if Griffith or Wagga home club, Karting SA, or Karting Tasmania. Entry **$140** (includes $45 levy). Entire-series entrants cannot change class after Round 1 close.

## Extra divisions

**Victorian Combined Masters** and **Ladies Masters:** KA100 **165 kg**; all other engines **172 kg** (TaG Restricted + KA3 mix).
