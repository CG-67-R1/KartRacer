# Karting helmets

MSA: full-face, unbreakable eye protection, **DOT / SNELL / ECE / CMR** as approved for cars, bikes or karts. **CMR strongly recommended under 16** (compulsory in much of Europe). **No helmet older than 10 years** from manufacture. Labels must be visible. Loose straps (helmet, neck brace, suit) can earn a **black-and-orange** until secured.

## Specs

| Spec | Notes |
|---|---|
| DOT | US street minimum, self-cert, little change since 1972 |
| ECE | European street motorcycle — not the kart focus |
| SNELL | Most common kart cert in SA; independent lab |
| CMR / CMS | SNELL junior, lighter to cut rotational mass. CMS/CMR sizes to **59 cm** |

CIK-FIA list (Aug 2023 on the source page) names which SA / K / CMR labels are currently valid — **check the live list**. SA = auto racing + flammability; K = kart (no fire test). Certs expire.

Shell: fibreglass / composite / carbon. **EPS liner is single-impact** — replace after a crash; do not poke the foam. Labels often under the padding or on the D-rings. Soap and water only; store in a bag, cool, dark.

## Fit

Measure ~25 mm above the brows at the largest rear point (with balaclava if you race in one). Snug on, skin on the brow should move with the helmet. Chin-strap tight: you must not roll it off or over the eyes. **Double-D:** through both rings, back through the lower, snug, snap the tail. Release = red tab, then unthread.

Common shop brands: ARAI, SPARCO, Headtec.
