# Wet weather

Rain tyres first. Drive **off** the oily rubbered line: square corners, cross the dry line. Lean out to load the outside front. Visor: Rain-X outside, anti-fog inside. Rain suit over the race suit.

## Chassis

- Front track **maximum** (spindle extensions if the maker has them)
- Rear track **minimum**
- Front ride height **maximum**
- **Maximum caster**; add camber if you can
- Higher pressures (wets struggle to temp)
- Medium axle if time
- **Remove** torsion bars
- Seat rear **~25 mm** higher (or a folded towel)
- Rear ride height **maximum**
- Shield the rear rotor (number panel ahead of the caliper)
- Tape pod holes
- WD-40 on the ignition
- Drain holes in the seat
- Airbox water deflector **only if class-legal**

## Gearing

With rain tyres, **+3 or more rear teeth**. Wetter = more teeth. As it dries and corner speed rises, remove teeth.

## Why caster

Slow corners make inside-rear lift harder. Extra caster increases jacking and front grip.
