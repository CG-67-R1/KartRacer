# Rotax exhausts and matting

Numbers: [`data/rotax-hardware.json`](../data/rotax-hardware.json). Muffler is **competitor-maintained** (not a sealed engine part). Confirm current part numbers.

Jnr/Snr/DD2 use a **tuned** pipe (header / diffuser / baffle / stinger). Micro/Mini are a different, non-tuned layout with the same *style* can. Life of the wool is ~**20 hours**. Burned mat → leaner, popping, louder. Pre-EVO it also opened the PV early.

## Legal masses (example at scrape)

| | New size | New mass | Used min | Part |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Micro / Jnr / Snr / DD2 | 480×270 mm ±10 | 176–238 g | **140 g** | 297982 |
| Mini | 490×180 mm ±10 | 119–163 g | **110 g** | 297985 |

Mini stinger OD is larger, so the wrap is narrower (180 vs 270).

Stingers: **273210** Jnr/Snr/DD2 (perforated to the engine end); **273211** Mini (X on flange, welded engine-end); **273212** Micro (unperforated length `d` at the engine end).

Micro/Mini header: **ball** socket, **1.0 mm** wall, new hook shape. Old 1.5 mm / old hooks are illegal.

## Swap procedure (tool checklist)

1. Drill rivets **4 mm**, top first. Hold the mandrel so it does not spin. Jnr/Snr/DD2: unhook springs and rotate the can for the underside rivets. Micro/Mini are one-piece — reach under. Gloves if hot.
2. Twist-pull the stinger from the 90° outlet with pliers.
3. Wind the old wool out in **one piece**; hook leftovers with threaded rod + torch. Do not leave ash in the can.
4. Inspect can and stinger for cracks. Roll new wool **tight**. Slide in rotating the same way it was rolled. Soft mallet on the last millimetres.
5. Line the outlet. Rules allow **M4 × ≥10 mm** + nyloc instead of rivets. Ream holes if needed.

## EGT

Welded socket **50–80 mm** from the header **ball**. Useful for jetting vs a known baseline. **Illegal in Rok**.
