## LeCont LOH / SVB spec (dry — KA2, TaG 125, Junior Performance)

Sizes 10x4.50-5 / 11x7.10-5. Service pressure 0.7 bar ±0.3; max 4.0 bar.
Tread depth 3.6 mm ±0.5; weight ~1100 g; 2-ply. Hardness **51.4 IRHD ±5**
(tender class: Hard, medium–high grip; 150 km durability target; 49% left
after 130 km). KA recommended cold pressure: **8.5–10 psi**.

## LeCont LPM / SVC spec (dry — X30, Rok GP/Senior, KZ2, Open Perf)

Sizes 10x4.50-5 / 11x7.10-5. Service pressure 0.7 bar ±0.3; max 4.0 bar.
Tread depth 3.6 mm ±0.5; weight ~1100 g; 2-ply. Hardness **46.0 IRHD ±5**
(tender class: Medium, high grip; 100 km durability target; 38% left after
75 km — softest and fastest-wearing dry in the set). KA recommended cold
pressure: **8.0–9.5 psi**.

## Maxxis MW21 / MW22 (wet — Cadet: MW21 front 10x4.00-5, MW22 rear 11x5.00-5)

Service pressure 1.0 bar ±0.3; spec-paper recommended inflation 0.6 bar;
max 4.0 bar. Tread depth 4.5 mm ±0.5; hardness 50 IRHD ±5 (Shore 55; SOFT/
MEDIUM classification). Weights: 980 g front / 1150 g rear. The KA FAQ
confirms: Cadet wet = MW21 front + MW22 rear; all other classes = SV1.

## Maxxis Sport / Super Sport (4SS Jr/Sr per 2026 Manual; social)

2026 KA Manual class table lists Maxxis Sport as the 4SS Jr/Sr dry.
Community-reported working pressures ~10–12 psi — NOT official; treat as
anecdote until St George Karts publishes guidance. (Historical note: at the
2021 tender 4SS was on Vega VAH under a separate contract; the 2026 Manual
table in `kb-au-rules` governs.)

## Recommended cold pressures (cited summary)

| Tyre | Cold psi | Source |
|---|---|---|
| LeCont LH03 AUS | 9.5–11.0 | KA notice + International Karting (importer) |
| LeCont LOH (SVB) | 8.5–10 | KA notice |
| LeCont LPM (SVC) | 8.0–9.5 | KA notice |
| Maxxis Cadet M190D | 0.6 bar (~8.7 psi) spec-paper recommended | KA spec paper |
| LeCont SV1 wet | 0.9 bar (~13 psi) service ±0.3 | CIK homologation form |
| Maxxis MW21/22 wet | 1.0 bar service / 0.6 bar recommended | KA spec paper |

These are starting points; optimum varies with track and weather (KA's own
caveat). The pyrometer loop in `tyre-pressure-weather-troubleshooting-guide.md`
takes over from these baselines.

## Homologation periods

KA contract and spec papers: **2022–2026** (LeCont + Maxxis). SV1 CIK form
validity 3 years from CIK homologation (053-TW). Expect a new tender for 2027+
— recheck karting.net.au/administration/technical each season.
