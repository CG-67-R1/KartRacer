# Rotax power valve

Numbers: [`data/rotax-hardware.json`](../data/rotax-hardware.json). **Senior Max and DD2 only.** Legal valve is the **EVO electronic** unit.

## What it does

Blade in the exhaust port. Down = shorter port (low-rpm torque). Up = taller port (mid/top). Pre-EVO opened from **exhaust** pressure vs a spring, target ~**7500 rpm**, and drifted with matting, jetting, and weather. EVO still uses bellows pressure, but **crankcase + one-way hose → magnetic valve → ECU**. Open rpm is **repeatable**.

| Class | Loom grounded | Loom open |
|---|---|---|
| Senior | **7600** | **7900** |
| DD2 | **8800** | **9100** |

Pick with the stopwatch.

## Day check (free movement)

Cover off, adjuster + O-ring out, two Taptite out, lift cover and spring. Grip piston hex; it must slide freely. Sticky → loosen housing Allens, realign, or pull the blade and de-carbon the slot and impulse bore.

## Strip lengths (tool)

- Stud in blade + Loctite: **72.0 ± 0.3 mm** from deepest blade curve to rear of stud.
- With plastic piston on: **81.6 ± 0.3 mm**.
- Blade **chamfer down** (toward bottom of engine).
- New gasket must not cover the impulse bore or pinch the blade.
- Oil seal, bellows bead fully in groove, inner then outer hose spring. Cable-ties can stretch the outer spring; cut them off.
- EVO adjuster: nip the O-ring. It does **not** set rpm (pre-EVO did).

Full strip ≤ **3 months**. Race-morning free-play check every day. Keep Scotch-Brite off rubber.
