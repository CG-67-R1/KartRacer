# KA licences, ages, grades

Full tables: `data/licences.json`.

## Get licensed

1. Join an affiliated club (Club Finder + KOMP).
2. Apply in **KOMP** (`portal.karting.net.au`) — membership and licence in one place.
3. Complete safety training. D / 8-Day (and listed E) complete an **Observed Driving Session** before a Meeting.
4. Under 18: Participant’s Licence on the parent/guardian.

No KA licence → no driving on a KA-licensed track.

**Types:** competition (Cadet 9 / Cadet 12 / Junior / Senior, grades A–D plus E and 8-Day), recreational E, Vintage, Pit Crew, Participant (free), KAC, International, Dealer Trade. **8-Day:** one per person per year.

## Ages (NCR, not marketing blurbs)

| Level | Min | Max |
|---|---|---|
| Cadet 9 | 6th birthday practice; **7th to race** | 10th birthday |
| Cadet 12 | 9th birthday | 13th birthday |
| Junior | 11th birthday (KA2 / Junior Max / Junior Performance from **12th**) | 16th birthday |
| Senior | **15th** new; **14th** if moving up from Junior | none |

Marketing pages say Cadets 6–12, Juniors 12–15, Seniors 16+, Masters 30+. Use the table above for eligibility; class chapters add Masters 40+ (KA3 / TaG Restricted) or 32+ (DD2 / Rok GP).

**Moves:** Cadet 9 → 12 from 9th, must by 10th. Cadet 12 → Junior from 11th, must by 13th.

## Grades

D → C: **4** endorsed Meetings. C → B: **6** Meetings or top-5 National Championship/Series. B → A: top-3 State Championship (Junior or Senior as applicable). Apply via KOMP to the State Secretary.

**P plate** on D and 8-Day.

**National** Cadet 9/12 and KA3: minimum **C**. KA2 / Junior Max / X30 / Rotax 125 / TaG 125 / DD2 / Rok GP: minimum **B**. TaG Restricted at State Championship+: typically **maximum C** (40+ may hold A/B). 4SS Super: typically **maximum B** unless 40+.

VIC/SA: Cadet/Junior D Grade need **2× D→C endorsements** before Zonal / State Series / Cup / Trophy.
