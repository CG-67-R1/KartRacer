# Protests and appeals

Fees in [`data/sporting-msa.json`](../data/sporting-msa.json) are **MSA examples**. Confirm current GCR before you write a cheque.

## Protest

Right sits with the **Entrant** (or an official) who is aggrieved. Lodge with Stewards / COC / Secretary **with the fee**. Usual clock: **30 minutes** from the act (full list **GCR 200**).

| Level | Fee (example) |
|---|---|
| Regional | **R2 000** |
| National | **R5 000** |

Writing: name, address, grounds, signature. Time of receipt noted. Technical protest **lists the parts**. **One protest per kart** — no globals. Stewards may demand a **cost deposit**. **No lawyers** at the hearing.

**GCR 203 inadmissible:** refused entry; collective (more than one signature); late (unless GCR 200 note); bad faith / frivolous / vexatious.

Room: chair, steward(s), COC, TC/scrutineer if technical, competitor + parent/guardian + entrant, witnesses, sometimes a Judge of Fact. No spectators. Record names and objections, then evidence and cross-exam. Finding oral + written. Success: fee back minus ~**10%** admin. Bad-faith protest (**GCR 206**): fees forfeit **and** further penalty.

Observation form ≠ protest (`race-day`).

## Appeal

Except where the book says **final**. Not against a Judge of Fact. Pay all outstanding fines first. **Leave to appeal** to MSA: usually **Wednesday COB** after the meeting, or **7 working days** if results were amended.

MSA path (example): leave **R5 000** → if granted another **R5 000** (**R10 000** to COA). Reply ~**7 days**. Still **no lawyers** at COA.

**NCOA** (gross miscarriage or wholly wrong penalty): leave **R10 000**, then formulated appeal in **7 working days** + **R20 000** (**R30 000** total). Lawyers discouraged but allowed if MSA is told **≥ 7 days** before the hearing.
