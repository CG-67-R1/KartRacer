# Tillotson HW-49A (OK-N)

OK-N uses a **diaphragm** carb with a **butterfly**, not a Dellorto slide. Pump + pump diaphragm + **metering diaphragm**. Works in any orientation (same family as a chainsaw carb).

Inspect diaphragms/gaskets every weekend; replace at least once a season. Flat metering convolution or sagging pump diaphragm = replace. Kits: DG-2HW (diaphragm/gasket), RK-11HW (plus needle/seat), 233-721P (seat/needle).

## Pop-off

~**80 kPa** then a slight drop that **holds**. If it keeps falling, the needle leaks. Check every race day. Change **OEM springs** (factory 37 g; range ~26–48 g) rather than cutting/stretching. High pop-off: revs hang on lift. Low: slow to take load.

Metering lever must sit **flush** with the body (bend carefully). Too low = dead throttle; too high = needle won’t seal.

## Needles

**L** (engine side) = low rpm. **H** (filter side) = high rpm. In = leaner, out = richer. Baseline if unknown: **L 2½ turns**, **H 2 turns** from lightly seated. Do not jam tapers into aluminium.

Butterfly idle screw: **cushion only** — do not set trolley idle with it (leans in corners / on lift). Set true **100% WOT**; past-centre butterfly is less than full throttle.

## Track

Warm first. **L:** hesitate + smoke out of corners → lean a little; delayed then sharp / ping → richer. **H:** cannot reach limiter at end of straight → lean H. Airbox test: block one inlet — faster = was lean, slower = was rich.

Don’ts: overtighten cover screw (cracks), sharp scrapers on the body, cross-thread the seat, overtighten needles.
