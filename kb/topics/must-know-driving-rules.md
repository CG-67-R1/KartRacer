# Must-know driving rules

Two books: **technical** (kart/engine) and **sporting** (driver, team, family). Source examples are **MSA National Karting 2023** — confirm current series regs. Penalties are harsher if an advantage was gained.

Knowing the rules also lets you file an Observation Report or Protest. Wrong protests cost money.

## Formation lap and start

This is a **formation**, not a race-pace warm-up. Pole sets pack speed. Halfway, pole slows so the field bunches.

- **In position by the red line** (~80 m before start; Killarney example: just past Pit Bend). Late = start from the back.
- **Constant speed from the red line** until lights out / start flag. No overtaking, no improving position.
- **Empty grid slot:** leave a kart-length space. Filling it is jumping two places.
- **Tramlines:** stay centred between the two thick white lines after the red line. Do not touch them until lights out.
- **False start:** multiple flags; hands up; slow; reform before the red line.
- Watch marshals — they may send another formation lap.

## On-track offences (steward language)

| Offence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cut-in** | Outside kart squeezes the inside kart off. Corner belongs to the inside kart if its fronts are at least level with the outside kart's sidepod (not a late dive). |
| **Edge-into** | Attacker leaves the circuit/kerb to pass where there is no room. Never allowed. |
| **Push-out** | Leader squeezes a kart that is alongside (fronts to sidepod) off the driveable surface. |
| **Bump** | Rear contact that upsets the kart ahead, especially into braking. Even a tap that costs a place is typically the bumper's fault. |
| **Short-cut** | Leaving the black stuff / red-white kerbs. |
| **Zig-zag / weave** | More than two left-right moves on a straight. One defend + one for the corner is the limit. |
| **Flag disregard** | Worst class. Yellow = danger, no overtaking until flags end. Ignoring flags / passing under yellow = **exclusion**. |
| **Technical non-compliance** | Track width, underweight, illegal parts/fuel, unchecked second-hand kit. |
| **Other / conduct** | Parent/guest behaviour is proxy for the driver. Alcohol in the pits on race day is treated as the driver's. |

## Nosecone and weigh

A triggered nosecone at the scales is an **automatic 5-second** penalty. After the chequered flag, leave a gap in the pits so you do not tap it. You must weigh to be classified.

## Yellow-flag example

Slow, be ready to stop, **do not overtake** until you are past the yellow zone. "I didn't see it" is not a defence.

MSA (South Africa) publishes current technical and sporting regs — it is the competitor's job to stay current.
