# TAS 2026 State Regulations

Final V1. Overrides: `data/state-overrides.json` → `tas`.

## General

- Min **4** entries to constitute a class.
- Club: all national classes permitted. Vintage by era only.
- **TaG Restricted at State Cup / Championship:** B Grade and above Senior **not permitted** (Class Rules Ch 12 apply at the Championship).
- Club: all grades in TaG Restricted are eligible for points/awards.

## TAS extras

**KA3 Masters:** Light **165**, Heavy **172**.

**KA3 Junior feature** (one-off at Zonal / State Cup / Trophy): KA100 **141 kg**.
