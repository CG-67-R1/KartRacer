# Tyre temperature lookup

Read **outside / middle / inside** as soon as the kart stops. Band: **75–85 °C**. Danger: **~95 °C**.

Match front-vs-rear averages. Ignore L/R unless the split is huge (bent?).

## Rear

| Pattern | Action |
|---|---|
| Cold middle | Raise pressure |
| Hot middle | Drop pressure |
| Hot inner | More rear transfer (narrow rear, stiffen rear) |
| Hot outer | Less transfer (widen rear, soften rear) — may hop |

## Front

| Pattern | Action |
|---|---|
| Hot inner | Less negative camber (or check caster) |
| Hot outer | More negative camber |
| Hot middle, even edges | Drop pressure |
| Cold middle, hot edges | Raise pressure |

Softer pressure increases roll → you may need more negative camber after a pressure change.

## Wear / surface

- Even sandpaper grain: good
- Smooth / no grain: too cold, not working
- Aggressive strips on an inner edge: too much caster/camber or that edge is overheating
- **Cone (inner gone, outer unused):** rear = more transfer; front = camber/caster/front width — fix before the profile is ruined
- **Blisters:** overworked, too hot

## Kart-setup heat shortcuts

- Hot centre → too much pressure
- Cool centre → too little pressure
- Both front inners hottest → too much negative camber
- Both front inners hottest **plus** turn-in oversteer → too much caster
- Front inners too cool → more negative camber
