# Centrifugal clutch

Shoes or a slotted disc are thrown out against a **drum that freewheels on a needle bearing**. Force scales with mass and with **rpm²**.

## Engagement

Old all-class cap: **4000 rpm** max. Rok: typically **3500 rpm**. Engagement = rpm at which an **unmanned** kart starts to move on the flat. Now in class technical regs, not the old SSR line.

Pinion tooth counts are class-specific — some free, some fixed.

## By family

- **Comer C50:** three sprung shoes slide out along a Y-boss.
- **Kid / Mini Rok:** disc with three slots; shoes bend outward at a weak section.
- **Rotax Max:** similar disc principle. Old and new drums both legal; new drum has an outer rib (still cracks — inspect the **inner** wear face). Same clutch across Rotax classes; **DD2** disc is ~3 mm thicker and the drum is taller. DD2 clutch lives inside the gearbox — harder access.

## Service

Lock the crank (proper tool, or last-resort screwdriver in the ring-gear teeth) before undoing the drum nut. Remove chain guards and chain first (except the buried DD2).

Friction face must stay **dry**. Tiny needle-bearing weep is allowed (see Rotax picture in the source). **Rotax O-ring between drum and needle must stay fitted.**

Strip, clean, inspect for cracks and legal dimensions on a regular interval. Grease the needle **sparingly** — excess grease on the drum inside is a fail.
