# Fees, protests, penalties

Tables: `data/fees-penalties.json`.

## Money (2026 national list, GST incl. where applicable)

- Organising permits: AKC $2360; National Series $1762; National Cup $1545; state/club set by SKA.
- 8-Day licence $115. International Driver $1145. KAC $25. Participant $0. Dealer $750. Photographer $212.
- **TDF levy $3** every entry.
- Protest: $700 national championship/series/cup; **$150** other.
- Appeal tribunal: $900 state/club; **$4750** national. AMSAC via Motorsport Australia.

## Penalty ladder

Reprimand → points (that driver only) → time/place → disqualify section / event / **meeting** → fine → suspension / exclusion. Unpaid fines accrue interest and block the licence.

**Restrictor infringement** example in the book: meeting disqualification.

2026: hearings should record audio (AI transcription recommended). Infringement Notice can be accepted without a full hearing.

Protests and appeals: General Rules Ch 12–14 — use the current Manual for windows and forms, not memory.
