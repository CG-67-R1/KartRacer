# Replacement of bearings

Three replacement classes:

1. **Must be same-kart OEM** — bodywork, bumper, spoiler. No brand-X panel on brand-Y chassis.
2. **OEM of some homologated system** — e.g. a complete CIK brake set (master + calipers + rotors) may replace another.
3. **Free** — fasteners, seat, caster/camber pills, **bearings**.

## DOF

A free body has 6 DOF. A deep-groove ball bearing leaves **rotation about one axis**. Rear **inserts** add a spherical OD so the inner can take a little **misalignment** (2 DOF). Rose/Heim ends: spherical inner in a non-metal cup; typically 2 DOF once the nut kills axial slide (3 possible).

## Seals (add suffix to the SKF number)

| Suffix | Type | Friction | Kart use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Z / ZZ / 2Z | Steel shield, gap to inner | Zero | **Preferred** (cheap, holds grease) |
| RSL / 2RSL | Non-contact rubber | Low | Next choice |
| RST / 2RST | Contact lip | Higher torque | Rarely used on a kart |

Open bearings belong **inside** an oil-fed crankcase, not on the axle.

## Fit rules (tool)

- Measure **ID / OD / width** on the part you are replacing before buying. Cassette bore usually follows axle OD; **some 1050 non-DD2** chassis are the exception. **50 mm axle + 80 mm OD** is an uncommon import (FSK-type).
- Inserts are **asymmetric** (grub-screw boss). Fit **both long sides inboard** or **both outboard** — that stack changes **axle stiffness**.
- Loosen **cassettes in the uprights** before sliding an axle. Hammering = bearings not coaxial. Spherical OD only forgives misalignment **one way**.
- Grubs: Loctite **on the threads only**, not the point. They will **brinnell** the axle — that is how they locate.
- Front hubs: **four** common sizes — measure before ordering.
- Stub/kingpin: **8 mm or 10 mm** bolt → two bearing sizes.
- Tie-rods: **190–290 mm**, hex (or turned flats), **LH + RH** M8×1.25. LH end has a **groove** on the OD.

Site tables for exact SKF numbers are images — record the number off the old bearing plus shield suffix.
