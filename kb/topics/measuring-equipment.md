# Measuring equipment

Wrong number at tech = exclusion. Use **this year’s** class spec, not memory.

## Tape

Accuracy class marked in the first **100 mm**:

| Class | Error over 10 m |
|---|---|
| I | ±1.1 mm |
| II | ±2.3 mm |
| III | ±4.6 mm |

Unmarked = unknown. Class II (typical Stanley/Ross) is **< ±0.2 mm** over an axle length — enough. Blade must **fit inside the axle bore**. Damaged claw or a slammed rewind kills accuracy.

**Rear track without a jig:** tape **through the axle**, clipboard on the far tyre, steel rule on the near tyre, read where tape crosses the rule.

## Vernier / digital / dial

Outside / inside / depth. Digital: zero with jaws closed. Dial: bezel to zero. Vernier 20-div = **0.05 mm**; 50-div = **0.02 mm** (prefer 50). Light constant thumbwheel pressure. Jaws **square** to the feature (else OD/depth read large, ID reads small). Lock screw only snug.

## Spring caliper (comparator)

Set against a **locked vernier**, not as a measuring tool. Example: Bambino venturi max **12.1 mm** — set vernier, rock the spring caliper over-centre on the jaws, then rock in the bore. Less resistance on an **internal** check → likely **oversize**.

## Micrometer

Clean faces. Close on ratchet; must read **zero**. Example read: 7 mm barrel + 0.14 mm thimble = **7.14 mm**. Lock only while transferring a size to a spring caliper.

## Telescopic gauge

Light lock, into hole, **tilt**, release, **rock once over centre**, remove, measure with light caliper pressure. Repeat. Extra rocks **undersize**. Not rocked / handle not level → **oversize** (a true 12.00 mm hole will read high).

## Squish

Solder through the plug hole, rotate past TDC, measure **~1 mm from the crushed end**. Do not squash the wire in the vernier. **Not used on Bambino.** Carbon may not be removed to “make” min squish.

- Kid/Mini Rok bore ~**42 mm**; Rotax/other Rok ~**54 mm**. Bent leg slightly **longer than half bore** so the end hits the wall.
- Ports usually fore-aft; measure **90°** (gudgeon line).
- Trim solder **square with a knife**, not side-cutters.
- Turn: clutch pinion nut, ring-gear, or DD2 **8 mm** bolt in the crank after the coin-slot plug.
- Left vs right often differ. Record both.

## Feeler

Plug gap and **Rok/Comer port timing** (blade **width** and **angle** are in the class tech). Calibrate feel on a vernier locked at the legal max. Stack **adjacent** 0.05 mm blades.
