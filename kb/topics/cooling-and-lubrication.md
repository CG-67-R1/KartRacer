# Cooling and lubrication

Numbers: [`data/rotax-hardware.json`](../data/rotax-hardware.json).

2-stroke **thermal** efficiency ≈ **20%** (port timing + scavenge). ~**80%** of fuel energy is heat: ~**40%** out the pipe, ~**35%** into the cooling system, remainder friction / pump / noise. **Mechanical** efficiency (brake / indicated) is a different number, typically ~**80%**. Combustion gas > **1500 °C** — above the melting point of the head/barrel if heat stays put. Oil film then cooks → carbon → **seize**.

## Air vs water

| | Air | Water (forced) |
|---|---|---|
| How | Fins radiate; speed adds convection | Pump in the **cold** leg (rad bottom → engine). Hot water to rad top |
| Who | Comer C50, Kid Rok, Mini Rok | All Rotax; Rok OKJ & DVS |
| Pump | — | Rotax ~**22 L/min @ 11 000 rpm**. Rok: axle-driven |
| Mass / frost / leaks | Lighter, no jackets | Heavier; frost and hose risk |
| Tune | Keep fins clear | **Flap** (Rotax) or **curtain** (Rok) over the core to hit WT |

Thermo-syphon is too slow for a kart. Target Rotax WT ~**50 °C** (`eyeballing-temperatures`).

## Oil

Total-loss. Almost all mix is burned. Only a little of **20–50 ml oil per litre of fuel** stays in the crankcase. Classes mandate **synthetic** and a ratio (examples: Bambino/Rok **25:1**, Rotax **50:1** — confirm). Castor gums and soots more; animal fats fail with heat. Friction losses rise ~**with rpm²**.

Modes: boundary (asperity contact), full-film (pump or hydrodynamic wedge), mixed. Oil also **seals** ring/land.

## Race-day water checks

Level + cap + O-ring; recheck **hot**. Clamp tightness. Leaks at hoses, core, pump joints, casing. Bent fins: long-nose straighten. Dirt: **compressed air, never a pressure washer**. Off-road: inspect core. At rebuild: pump gears, shaft, impeller.
