# Eyeballing the temperatures

Temps are a tool for longevity, economy, or power — not a substitute for the stopwatch.

| Sensor | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **WT** | Water jacket | Allowed widely; Rotax may use WT + EGT (max two sensors) |
| **EGT** | Header gas | Type-K thermocouple, fast, to ~1100 °C. Weld a nipple **50–80 mm** from the header ball joint |
| **CHT** | Under spark plug | Rare in karts: vibration, lag, ambient wash |

Kid Rok / Mini Rok / OKJ: often **no** temp sensors. DVS Regionals/Nationals: WT obligatory.

WT = resistance bulb. EGT = Chromel/Alumel voltage.

## Water temp

Cooler water → less friction, higher mechanical efficiency. **Rotax example target ~50 °C.** Micro/Mini often run **80–90 °C** because of small radiators — the principle still holds. Control with radiator flap/curtain; tape the core if rules allow (some ban tape you can peel mid-race).

## Lambda and EGT

Stoich petrol **14.7:1**. Lambda λ = (actual A/F) / 14.7. Example: 13.5:1 → λ = 0.92.

Best **power** on these 2-strokes is usually **richer than stoich**, about **λ 0.86** (A/F ~11.5–13.0, λ 0.78–0.88). Much richer: unused fuel/oxygen, exhaust **pop**. **Micro/Mini** often want λ near **1.0 or a bit leaner**.

At peak power, EGT is **below** the peak of the EGT curve (excess fuel cools the gas). The **same EGT** exists on the **lean** side at much less power. Do not chase a number blindly.

Best process: dyno WOT max-power EGT under known atmosphere, then match that EGT on track by jet/needle — you are matching **A/F**, not worshipping EGT. Probe **depth and distance** must match or you are comparing apples to oranges. A whole lap averages nearer λ 1.0 because you are not WOT everywhere.

RAD/jetting is the companion tool (`jetting-and-air-density`). The stopwatch still wins.
