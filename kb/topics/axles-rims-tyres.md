# Axles, rims and tyres

The rear axle is a cantilever spring. Stiffness (material + **ID^4**) controls how fast the inside rear lifts and returns. Hollow axles are lighter and springier than old solid Bambino bars.

## Axle OD by chassis / class

| Group | Wheelbase | Typical axle OD |
|---|---|---|
| Bambino | 77 cm | 25 mm hollow (Topkart 19 mm ID or EMR 18 mm ID, ~950 mm long) |
| Kid/Mini Rok, Micro/Mini Max | 95 cm | 30 mm |
| CIK / Rotax 105 cm, most classes | 105 cm | 50 mm |
| DD2 gearbox | 105 cm | 40 mm |

**30 mm tech limits (exclusion if wrong):** mass **2900 g ± 10 g**, length **960 mm ± 10 mm**, wall thickness per class / SSR / CIK.

## Rear track vs grip

Wider rear (more overhang past the bearing) = more axle bend = **softer** = harder to lift inside rear = more slide / oversteer / less grip.

**Max overall rear width** (outside of tyres/rims, not centre-to-centre):

- Bambino, Micro Max, Mini Max, Mini Rok: **1100 mm**
- Jnr/Snr Max, DD2, OKJ, OKN, KZ: **1400 mm**

## Rims and bolt patterns

- Front DSM: bearings in the rim, one nut on the stub.
- Hub-mount: 3 × 8 mm studs.
- **Metric:** 40 mm centre, 58 mm PCD
- **Rotax front:** 55 mm centre, 67 mm PCD

Magnesium: lighter, softer, faster heat in/out, ~2× aluminium price. Low-volume rims hold less air → less circumference growth as they heat.

Wider rim (within class limits) = less sidewall roll = **less grip**. Rear: longer hub or hub further inboard stiffens that axle end (**more grip**). Sliding the hub outboard exposes more axle (**less grip**). Front: hubs further outboard **add** front grip (5/10 mm spacers).

Hubs: aluminium or magnesium; front 17 or 25 mm bearings; rear sized to axle; lengths ~40–105 mm. Longer hub ≈ more grip.

## Tyres

All race kart tyres are **5"**. Follow the sidewall **direction arrow** (LeVanto logo is not an arrow). Wrong rotation on Mojo/Bridgestone is a technical offence. Factory says the arrow is safety (overlap peel + wet water path), not a performance cheat.

Slicks: max rubber + mutual support. Wets: grooves dump water and raise unit pressure.

**Rear circumference mismatch** on a live axle binds the kart (heat, wear, lost straight-line speed). Mfr diameter tolerances ~±5 mm (Mojo), ±6 mm (LeVanto), ±7 mm (Bridgestone). Measure with a cloth tape. Example fix: +**0.5 bar per 2 mm** circumference deficit on the smaller tyre, sun ~20 min, cool, reset race pressure, re-measure. Do not exceed max inflation.
