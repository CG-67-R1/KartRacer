# Handling troubleshoot

**One change at a time.** Confirm it is not the driver. On a **950**, axle polarity can invert vs 1050 literature — if the book fails, try the opposite.

Distinguish **slide** (add grip) from **hop** (reduce grip).

## Front not gripping / understeer on entry

- Front hubs out one spacer each side
- If already max wide: restore start width, **add caster**, recheck toe/camber
- Rear pressure **+0.1 bar**
- Raise front ride height
- More toe-out
- More caster
- Ballast forward

## Rear sliding on entry / too much front bite

- Front hubs in one spacer
- Rear pressure **−0.1 bar**
- Ballast off the front
- Lower front ride height (recheck geometry)
- Less caster
- Less Ackermann
- Narrow rear track
- Raise rear ride height
- Loosen third bearing (if fitted)
- Stiffer axle *(1050 textbook — verify on 950)*

## Hopping

**Tall-driver violent hop:** lower rear ballast, seat back and down, lower rear ride height, softer axle, remove extra struts.

**Tyre chatter:** rear pressure **+0.1 bar**, narrower rear, stiffer axle.

## Not enough side bite (four-wheel slide)

- Stiffen / fit torsion bars
- Hot pressures **−0.1 bar**

## Too much side bite (not drifting)

- Loosen / remove torsion bars
- Pressures **+0.1 bar**

## Tight / understeer on exit

- Narrow rear track
- Rear pressure **+0.1 bar**
- Softer axle
- Lower rear ballast
- Drop to one strut per side
- Lower rear ride height

## Only wrong in one direction

- Corner weights unequal
- Bent / twisted chassis
- Asymmetric settings or pressures

## Darty on straights, dives into corners

- Too much toe-out
- Front hubs out at least one spacer

## Push/kick (apex, brake-to-throttle)

- Seat forward
- Narrow rear
- Widen front
- Raise rear pressures
