# Changing tyres

Tools: compressor, gauge, **bead breaker**, tyre tongs, valve key, tyre grease or dishwashing liquid.

## Off

1. Off the kart. Remove **bead screws** (deflates) or the valve.
2. Break both beads.
3. Rear of rim up (valve down). Tongs disc in; nylon clips away from you; pin in; open jaws — tyre pops off one side.
4. Flip on edge, crunch tyre onto the valve side, 45°, pull rim out.
5. Clean rims. Check valve stems and bead-screw O-rings (leaks).

## On

1. Grease both beads.
2. Knee-dimple the tyre opposite points.
3. Offer the **rear** of the rim (away from the valve) at 45°. **Get the rotation arrow right first.**
4. Flip, tongs on the rear side, open fully (tip the assembly if needed).
5. Start bead screws **one or two threads only** — too deep cuts the tyre on inflate.
6. Inflate until **two pops** (both beads). Immediately drop pressure (pull a bead screw or valve).
7. Tighten bead screws with O-rings seated. Re-inflate to race pressure. Soap-bubble the valve and screws.

## Arrow vs corner

Offering the rear of the rim into the tyre: arrow **clockwise** → that wheel is for the **RHS**; **anti-clockwise** → **LHS**.
