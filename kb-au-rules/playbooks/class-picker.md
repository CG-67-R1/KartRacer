# Pick an Australian class

Use `data/classes.json` + the driver’s **licence level** in `data/licences.json`. Then apply `data/state-overrides.json` if they race a named state series.

1. **Age 6–7** — practice only (Cadet 9 / 4SS Cadet). Race from 7th birthday.
2. **Under 10** — Cadet 9 (Mini Rok + 16 mm) or 4SS Cadet (100 kg). Social: MicroMax 110 kg.
3. **9–13** — Cadet 12 (Mini Rok 110 kg). Social: MiniMax 125 kg.
4. **11–16 first junior** — KA3 Junior (22 mm, 132/150) or 4SS Junior (130) or Yamaha Junior. Faster: KA2 (142, B Grade) or Junior Max (145, B Grade) from **12th** birthday.
5. **First senior** — KA3 Senior (150/170/190) or TaG Restricted (160/180/200) or 4SS Senior (140/155/170; SA Heavy **175**). 4SS Super if max B Grade (or 40+).
6. **Open 125** — TaG 125, X30, Rotax 125, Rok GP (all typically **B Grade**).
7. **Gearbox** — DD2 (173) or KZ2 (177) or state Open Performance table.
8. **40+** — KA3 Masters / TaG Restricted Masters (NSW splits Light/Medium). VIC Combined Masters 165/172. TAS KA3 Masters 165/172.
9. **Historic** — Vintage only (no racing).

Always re-check the meeting Supplementary Regulations and current Manual PDF.
