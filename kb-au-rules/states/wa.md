# WA 2026 State Regulations

V3, 4 August 2026. Overrides: `data/state-overrides.json` → `wa`.

## General

- Cadet / Junior / TaG Restricted: engines/restrictors sealed at State Series and below.
- Front-fairing kits + cameras at **all** State Series / Cup / Trophy classes **except WA Open Performance** (no drop-down bumpers).
- Club: all grades score in TaG Restricted and 4SS Senior.
- **Full Course Yellow mandatory** at State Series / Cup / Trophy.
- Competition numbers stay for the whole WA Karting Series once allocated.
- One reverse-grid heat possible at State/Club when fields are small (see regs).

## WA Karting Series

May offer: Cadet 9/12, KA3 Junior L/H, KA2, KA3 Senior Championship/Heavy, TaG Restricted L/M/Masters, TaG 125 L/H. Class needs **4** rounds to count. **Best 6** meetings + travel bonus. Winners may run black plate with gold diagonal.

## WA Open Performance (with Ch 17)

Min **Senior B**. PULP only. No supercharge. One carb per cylinder, butterfly bore ≤ 30 mm. Front brakes **and** bead retention if combined weight **> 170 kg**. Max **5 dry + 5 wet** tyres per meeting (national replacement rule off). Engine category (and weight) may change on the day if Stewards are told first. Club: KA1 / DD2 / KZ2 may run to their own class rules.

Weights (max cc / min kg): piston 125/120; air CIK 110/120; water CIK 100–110/140; 106–135/141 → 140; KF 141/150; DD2 125/155; Biland 250/150; Rok DVS SV 125/160; X30 Super 175/170; twin Clubman/KA100 210/165; twin air 210/175; twin water 210/180; MX gearbox 128/170; KZ 128/185; Super Shifter / Rok Shifter 175/190; twin TAG 255/180; twin KF TAG 250/195; twin Torini 430/160.
