# Technical — formula, fuel, tyres, oils, apparel

Numbers: `data/tyres-fuel-oils.json`. Homologation PDFs live on the national technical page (not copied here).

## Fuel (Technical Ch 3)

Allowed: **PULP** (RON ≥ 95), **E10 PULP**, **E85 PULP (Vintage only)**, **Elf LMS**, **Panta Kart 102**, plus any fuel named in Supplementary Regulations. Do not mix unlike fuels. 4SS / 4SS Super: **PULP only**. Rok GP: pump or unleaded racing fuel.

## Oils (tech page + homologation)

Unless the homologation says otherwise: Penrite 10 Tenths Race Castor 20W-40; Penrite KO2ST 2-stroke; or current **CIK-FIA** homologated lubricants.

## Tyres (Technical Ch 4)

KA-approved only, from official suppliers (LeCont via Patrizicorse; Maxxis via St George). Class chapter names the compound. **Air only.** No dry+wet mix. One set = 2F+2R. Clerk declares **Wet** before wets.

From first qualifying: **1 dry + 1 wet** (+ one replacement each if approved). Bead retention required on several 125 classes (KA2, Junior Max, X30, Rotax 125, TaG 125, DD2).

## Homologated engines (current tech list)

**2-stroke:** IAME KA100, X30, X30 Super / Super Shifter; PRD Galaxy (post-2019), Fireball; Vortex Mini Rok, DVS Jnr, DVS-SV, Rok GP, Rok Shifter; Rotax Mini/Junior/Senior MAX, DD2 + Appendix A.

**4-stroke:** Torini TC210, TX250.

**Expired but still usable where listed:** pre-2019 Galaxy, older Rotax papers, Briggs 206, Subaru KX21.

**TD sheets:** KZ2, KA1, KA2. Trophy Class has its own technical regs.

## Apparel (Technical Ch 7)

CIK-style helmet and suit rules in the Manual. **2026:** long-hair apparel requirement added. Cadet/Junior: **rib protector mandatory**; neck brace and chest protector recommended. Video camera mandatory at National Championship (and many Cups) in several classes.

## Scrutineering / weight

Kart + driver + equipment vs class minimum. Several heavy divisions also have a **max kart-only** weight (96–102 kg typical). Engines often **sealed** in Cadet / Junior / TaG Restricted at State Series and below. Two engines from first qualifying.

## Chassis formula (high level)

Cadet min wheelbase **880 mm** (Micro/MiniMax 890+). Junior/Senior typical **1000 mm**. Cadet State/National: max width **1100 mm**, tighter axle ID. CIK front-fairing mounting kit and rear wheel protection as specified.
