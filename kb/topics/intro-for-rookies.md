# Intro for rookies

Club process is similar everywhere. The ASN issues the rules. Register with the club and sign the indemnity before track time. Do a structured intro (circuit closed to others) before you spend heavily.

## Before you race

1. Intro / academy
2. Licence application (online after the **flag test**)
3. Race number (form or Rotax app)
4. Join the main club via the karting section and pay fees
5. Know `must-know-driving-rules`

Age sets the class (year-of-birthday windows). 2023 note: Clubmans from the year of the 12th birthday. Confirm current tables.

## Training list for new drivers

Apparel fit and care · **Rookie X** on the helmet for at least the first four races · flags · no weaving · look where you want to go · no intentional contact · concede the corner unless clearly alongside · no payback · race-day process · setup basics · scrutineering · grid from quali · transponder hire/buy · formation/start/tramlines · racecraft. Year one is a grounding year.

## Bambino

From the year you turn 5. 77 cm chassis, pedal extensions as needed. Comer 50cc pull-start; C52 (+2 cc) from 2022.

## Clubmans (WPMC regional, budget)

- MSA/CIK homologated chassis (current or previous)
- Any previously homologated 125 cc fixed-gear Rotax or Rok (Junior or Senior)
- Min weights (examples): Junior engine 158 kg · Senior 175 kg · DVS 190 kg
- Max **25 kg** ballast
- Tyres: Mojo D2/D3/D5, Bridgestone YLR, LeVanto KRT, VEGA XH3
- Any fuel/oil (unlike specified-pump classes)
- Break-out: Gold 42.500 s · Silver 44 s (Killarney examples). Quali break-out = 5-place grid drop race 1. Race break-out = 5 places that race + next race from the back. Silver into Gold for the season if they break 44 s
- No mandatory 5 s nosecone (older fixed cones allowed)
- **No timing devices** in quali or racing
- Tech usually safety-only unless competitors ask

## Buying kit

Most start used. Get the chassis measured and homologation checked — a few mm out ruins handling. **Safety kit should be new:** suit, gloves, boots, neck brace, helmet, 1.5 kg extinguisher. Fuel kit: 25 L can, class oil, 250 ml + 5 L measuring jugs. Small toolbox. Track storage / lock-up pits via the club waitlist.

Treat officials as volunteers. Related: `race-day`.
