# Axle removal and replacement

Same sequence for 30 / 40 / 50 mm. DD2 adds the overload clutch and has **no RHS insert** (axle lives in the gearbox hollow shaft). Leave the DD2 engine bolted unless you are aligning a new build.

## Parts

- **Hub / sprocket / brake carrier:** keyway + clamp slot + cap screw(s). Key stops rotation; clamp stops axial slide. Tight bore: tap a **flat screwdriver** into the slot after loosening the cap — do not over-spread.
- **Keys:** tall (deep axle groove) or short with **pins** (shallow drilled groove). Short keys on sprocket/brake vs long on hubs.
- **Insert:** typically **three grubs**. Loctite threads. They raise **burrs** — file before pulling the axle through a carrier.
- **DD2 clutch:** slips on overload. Hex on gearbox output. **Six M5** through the housing; clamp ring to the left. Torque **7 N·m**. Over-torque **cracks** the housing. Four bushings on the axle.

## Chain-drive remove (order)

1. Stand. Loosen hub caps, slide hubs (open slot if needed).
2. Lift keys with **side-cutters** along the axle as a lever.
3. Slack the engine clamp, drop the chain off pinion and rear, leave it on the axle. Guard off.
4. Slide sprocket carrier, pull its key.
5. **All** insert grubs (and the third inboard if fitted). Do not drop them.
6. Loosen brake-carrier cap. **Cable-tie the pedal to the bumper** so the disc stays in the caliper.
7. Drive axle **left → right** (brake → engine) with a **stepped drift** the same OD as the axle (holds the disc). Soft mallet if no drift. No steel hammer on the tube end.
8. When free, pull the brake key. **File grub burrs** before the last of the axle exits the sprocket side. Catch carrier + chain.

## DD2 remove

Steps 1–2, 5–6 only (no chain). One LHS insert. Then: loosen clamp-ring cap, slide ring toward the disc; **fully** undo the six M5 (nuts can stay on). Tap axle **right → left** until the clutch hex is free. Slide clutch + ring left to expose bushings. Then drive **left → right** as above; clutch/bushings/ring fall free.

## Refit

- Clean; light film (WD40-class) on the axle. Always enter from the **engine** side.
- DD2: past the gearbox hex → four bushings → clutch → clamp ring → disc + key → LHS insert. Engage hex, **7 N·m** on the six, then lock the ring.
- Chain: refit chain + sprocket + key as the axle comes through.
- **Centralise before any clamp or grub:** same measurement both ends of the axle to a fixed chassis point (cassette OD, insert bush, or main tube). Then bolt.
- Align sprocket to pinion **after** centralising.
- Rotate axle so grubs hit **old** dimples. Loctite on threads only.
- Cut the pedal tie. Set rear track; do not exceed class max (`chassis-setup.json`).

## New chassis / first axle

Cassette bolts in the **same ride-height holes** both sides. Inserts in cassettes with **caps loose** (spherical OD must swivel). Slide axle through both; jiggle until it spins with **zero bind**; **then** tighten cassette caps. Pull the axle and build it properly.

**DD2 first build:** engine loose. Axle through LHS insert + gearbox. Equal **wheelbase** left vs right (rear axle to front axle or kingpin). Steering **dead ahead** if using stub axles as the datum. Tighten engine, then cassette. Axle should then slide out freely before the clutch goes on. Unequal wheelbase = **crab**.
