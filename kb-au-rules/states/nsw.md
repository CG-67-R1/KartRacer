# NSW 2026 State Regulations

V3, 22 May 2026. Overrides: `data/state-overrides.json` → `nsw`.

## General

- Extra classes/divisions for State Series and below are in this file, not only the national book.
- Consolidation allowed; clubs may cut laps.
- Cadet / Junior / TaG Restricted: engines and restrictors **sealed**; restrictors checked before sealing. KZ2 may use CIK or listed exhaust at State Series and below.
- Reverse grid: **≤ 20** karts in the class at State/Zonal/Club — one heat, named in Supplementary Regulations.
- Club: **all licence grades** score points in TaG Restricted and 4SS Senior.
- Trophy Series: min **5** entries to constitute; no oversubscribe; entry **$195** ($45 series levy). Drop lowest round. Formats 1–12 for State Series/Cup/Trophy; 1–18 Club.

## Extra divisions (State Cup and below)

**TaG Restricted Masters Light / Medium:** all engines **160 / 180 kg** (national Masters is a single band).

**KANSW Combined** (TaG Restricted Ch 12 + KA3 Ch 6): max **B Grade Senior**.

Restricted Light kg: KA3 **153**, X30 **160**, Fireball **160**, Rotax SR2 **165**, SR3 **160**, SR4 **165**. Medium = +20. Heavy = +40. KA3 weight may be moved during the year for parity.

## Trophy Series classes (listed)

Cadet 9, Cadet 12, KA3 Junior Championship, KA2, KA3 Senior Championship, TaG 125 Championship, X30, Rotax Light, TaG Restricted Light, TaG Restricted Masters, KANSW Combined.
