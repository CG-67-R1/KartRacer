# Scrutineering, weight and fuel

Written for officials but competitors should self-check the same list. Rule stack, bottom-up if silent: class spec → provincial → SSR → GCR → CIK/FIA, plus circulars, SRs, COC bulletins.

Safety is still the **competitor’s**. Scrutineering is an extra check, not a warranty.

## Pass/fail highlights

- **Nosecone:** no extra ties except one loose tie on the clamp so it is not lost. **27 mm** gap pod-to-bumper (CIK). Worn/repaired cones fail (won’t pop off). Bambino / some Clubmans excepted
- Numbers: four plates, correct colours/size
- Kingpins: full-lock free, locknuts tight, steering-shaft retainer
- Nylocs you can turn by hand = fail (hammer-tap nylon is a temporary fix)
- Cast hubs: cracks fail. Wheels tight; “I loosen them for the trailer” is not accepted
- Tyres: class, condition, dimples visible, **correct rotation**
- Throttle return spring must snap shut
- Brakes: dual pedal–master link (rod + cable), pedal return spring, effective, pads/discs OK
- Magnetic steel chassis; no extra joints/dampers; no lightening holes
- Floor pan: rigid (not glass/Kevlar), no downward skirts
- Tank leak-free, hoses clipped, nothing dragging under the frame
- **Ballast:** bolts + 35 mm washers + nyloc; feel-tight. ≤2 kg → min 1 bolt; +1 bolt per 4 kg or part. Max **5 kg** per piece. No cable ties/tape
- Cameras: stock brackets only on pods, bumpers, bib, radiator top — **no helmet, no stalks**
- Side pods > **25 mm** off the ground
- Overflow bottles on fuel and coolant (not Bambino Comer tank)
- Transponder under bib, not ahead of the front axle
- Chain finger guard to the pinion/sprocket centreline
- Dry-cell battery only, restrained
- Extinguisher on the trolley: dry powder **≥ 1.5 kg**, inspected ≤12 months, name + number
- Apparel: no holes; helmet undamaged with legal label; boots cover ankles

Late scrutiny = pay the fine first. Log book stamped by the secretary before sign-off.

## After quali / during the day

Mark tyres (inner wall or barcode), engine seals, air filter, carb, muffler fasteners. Impound non-compliant parts. Incidental finds in a strip still count. Officials do not give “advice” that is really a protest.

Parc fermé: officials + competitor/entrant only. No tyre deflation. Do not touch karts until COC release.

## Weigh

Every exit: nosecone + scales. No driving over the scale. One reweigh if light; stay in the area. Min masses are in current SSRs (2023 table is on the source page — confirm live).

## Fuel (Digatron FT-64)

Accredited Fuel TC only. Can drain and refill with SR-specified pump fuel; competitor supplies **sealed** class oil.

Reference mixes (confirm SRs): Bambino **25:1** · Rok **25:1** · Rotax **50:1**. ~3 L pump fuel, 500 ml marked pots. Check reference during the day (temp drift).

Probe between scribed marks, not touching tank; dielectric ±**2** of sample; temp delta ≤ **2 °F**. Fail after a session = non-protestable exclusion for the heat. Prefer dummy-grid checks so they can remix. Illegal reading: Admission of Guilt + COC notice.
