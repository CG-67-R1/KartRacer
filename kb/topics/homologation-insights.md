# Homologation insights

Tool numbers: [`data/sporting-msa.json`](../data/sporting-msa.json). FIA → CIK (karting, 1962) → ASN (MSA in ZA).

CIK licences from **age 13**. Younger classes are ASN. Families: gearbox, direct drive, superkart. Groups (2-stroke):

| Group | Engine | Drive | Wheelbase |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 125 | gearbox | ~1050 mm |
| 2 | 125 | chain | ~1050 mm |
| 3 | 60 | chain (Mini) | ~950 mm |
| 4 | 250 | gearbox | superkart |

Group 1 examples: ~**170 kg** kart+driver, **0–100 ~3.5 s**, ~**180 km/h**. Group 4: ~**75 kW**, just over **200 kg**, enclosed body.

World Championship classes ≠ Rotax Max Challenge. WC Group 2: OKJ / OK; Group 1: KZ1 / KZ2. Rotax: Micro / Mini / Jnr / Snr / DD2 — same 125 architecture, power via restrictors, pipe, squish, PV.

**125 vs old 100:** 100s (“screamers”) > **20 000 rpm**, mean piston speed > **33 m/s** (F1 ~**26.5 m/s @ 15 000**). 125s: reeds, digital ignition, limiter, often balancer shaft, optional electric start + clutch or decompressor + push, water pump, PV on senior/gearbox. Ratios homologated.

## What homologation is

CIK confirms **enough units exist** and the part matches the group spec. Typical validity **3 years** (extendable). Rotax chassis certs often have **no end date**. Marking must be on the part. French-derived abbreviation stamps identify the equipment type.

Example **minimum production**: chassis / bodywork / brakes **75**; engine / carb / exhaust **50**; tyres **500**. Chassis cert lists tubes **> 150 mm** long (diameter), bend count, wheelbase, bodywork points, plate photo. Rotax series chassis need **CIK** marks except **DD2** (Rotax axle mount → Rotax homologation).

Tyres: manufacturer self-cert **250 km** (Option and Mini) or **150 km** (Prime) before cord — not “still race-winning.” Hardness **IRHD / ISO 48**. Pressures on the cert are **starts**, not a mandate. Class regs pick the tyre; the driver does not.
