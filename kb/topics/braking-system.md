# The braking system

Power cited: ~1.5 kW Bambino to ~24 kW DD2. All karts have a **rear disc**. Bigger karts add **two front discs** because brake dive unloads the rear and the fronts do most of the work.

Bambino: mechanical rear disc. Other classes: hydraulic. Larger karts: twin masters + **bias bar** (one pedal, split force). 2-pot or 4-pot calipers; ventilated or solid rotors. Every OEM homologates its own mix (CRG has ~6 current systems).

Pedal rod **higher** = heavier pedal. Master **higher** hole = more sensitive / lighter pedal. Spongy = bleed. Drag kills top speed.

## Pads

Modern pads are sintered composites (silicon, resins, aramid/Kevlar, etc.) on a backing plate. Compounds: soft / medium / hard (try Frixia if the OEM has no range).

**Bed-in is mandatory.** Overheated pads pit/fissure (resin boiled out) or glaze. **Do not de-glaze with emery** — silica embeds and scores the rotor. Scrap glazed/pitted pads.

Bed-in outline:
1. Clean caliper and rotor with brake/carb cleaner. Dry-cloth the new pads. No oil, no cleaner on pad faces. Pump to centre.
2. Five light stops from low speed (system check).
3. 50% revs, lift, light pedal 3 s × 10–12. Do not force the stop.
4. 75% revs, moderate pedal 5 s × 10–12. Never brake and accelerate together.
5. Cool on a slow in-lap. If pads come out, mark sides and refit the same way.

Goal: even transfer layer on the rotor.

## Fluids — do not mix bases

| Spec | Base | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| DOT 3 / 4 | Glycol | Clear–straw |
| DOT 5 | Silicone | Purple |
| DOT 5.1 | Hybrid, mostly glycol | Clear–straw |

Glycol eats paint and absorbs water. Silicone is paint-safe. Higher glycol DOT can replace lower glycol DOT, not the reverse. **Never mix silicone and glycol.** Use the OEM spec.

Examples: CRG = DOT 4. OTK BS1–BS4 (to ~2005) DOT 4; BS5/BS6 DOT 5; from ~2015 OTK uses **DOT 5.1**.

## Bleed (summary)

- **CRG:** reservoir to mark, hose on bleed nipple, hold pedal down, crack nipple, close **before** pedal returns, top up, repeat until firm / no bubbles. Some rears need both sides of the caliper.
- **OTK:** bleed tower in the 5 mm plug hole if you have one; gravity bleed at the caliper (8 mm or 3 mm Allen on older). No tower / no reservoir: pump **slowly** and never let the master empty.
- After F+R bleed: on the stand, set bias so fronts and rears just lock similarly by hand.

## Service

Every session: pads, rotor, hoses, level, pedal travel. Fluid ~**every 500 laps**. Opened bottles: 1–2 years max. Strip masters/calipers ~**every 1000 laps**; seal/rebuild kits exist for most systems.
