# Competition structure, starts, finish, consolidation

Levels and 2026 AKC rounds: `data/meeting-levels.json`.

## Levels (high → low)

National Championship (AKC) → National Series → National Cup → State Championship → State Series → State Cup → State Trophy → Zonal → Club → Social Karting Activity.

One KA licence works at affiliated clubs Australia-wide. Entries via KOMP calendar.

**2026 AKC:** Coffs Harbour, Ipswich, Townsville, Seymour, Bolivar.

## Consolidation

Seniors with seniors, juniors with juniors. Cadet 9 + Cadet 12 + 4SS Cadet may share a track. Slower class **at the rear**. Results and points **by class**, not overall. Club meetings may collapse to one weight division.

Typical stack: Cadet 12 in front of Cadet 9 / 4SS Cadet; KA3 Junior in front of 4SS Junior; 4SS Super in front of 4SS Senior.

## Start / finish notes

- Weaving to warm tyres allowed before the Slow Down line on the roll-up.
- After the Formation Line: no overtaking.
- Restart (green/yellow chevron) only before the leader completes lap 1.
- **Finisher:** under own power across the line, **≥ 75%** of the winner’s distance.
- Early/late chequered: race ends when it should have under the schedule.
- Red-flag declared race: classification from the penultimate lap. Stationary karts are not classified.
- One kart faces the starter → win by default.
- Points ties: random-grid heats use place counts then last section; qualifying grids use faster qualify (for grids) or last-section place (for final).

## Entries

Entry is a contract via KOMP. Under 18 needs a Participant’s Licence holder 18+. After close of entries, class changes follow the meeting regs (often locked).

## Social Karting

Appendix 1. MicroMax and MiniMax are **social only**. SKA inside 48 h of a State Series/Cup/Trophy/Zonal is usually limited to entered drivers in their entered class (VIC/SA spell this out).
