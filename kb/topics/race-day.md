# It's race day

Process for a club/national meeting (MSA-style). Read **that event's SRs**.

## Paperwork

Entry form + SRs come with the club mail. Know GCRs, SSRs, and class sporting/technical regs. SRs hold the timetable and extras (licence copy, etc.).

Enter early so organisers can combine classes and fix the timetable. Organisers may refuse an entry or cancel a thin class. Signing the entry confirms fitness, competence, and legality. Fees stick unless withdrawn within **2 working days** of close.

Race day: competitor (and parent/guardian if under 18) **sign on**, collect promoter decals, collect **two** parc-fermé armbands (mechanic + helper).

## Scrutineering

Self-scrutineer at home. GCR 113: entrant present, kart **clean**, **on time** (late = fine). Competitor present with suit, helmet, boots, gloves, extinguisher, **log book** (entry-fee stamp). Apply the scrutineering sticker before leaving parc fermé.

Mix fuel from the **SR-specified** pump as soon as you arrive. No spills. Closed container, cool pit. **GCR 123: no smoking** in pits, pit lane, pit wall, or fuel areas.

## Practice, briefing, quali

Use every free practice — last clean window for setup, jet, gear, pressure. Expect more changes as the track rubbers and humidity moves.

**Drivers' briefing is mandatory.** COC may require loose nosecones on the dummy grid or clutch-drum inspection (arrive early — refit is not 10 seconds). Dummy grid is also a common **fuel-test** point; fail = empty and remix. Only **water** in form-up; tools banned except a **pressure gauge**.

## During the day

After quali/weigh, scrutineers may **mark** parts. Changes only in front of them; old parts impounded — sign the form, collect at the end.

Watch the notice board / event WhatsApp. No sympathy for missed bulletins.

Nosecone checked every time you leave the track. **Do not fix it on track or toward the scales** — 5 s is better than **exclusion** for tampering.

Underweight: **one** reweigh; driver stays at the scales. Nosecone and scale areas are **no-mechanic** zones (Bambino sometimes excepted). Spot tech checks after heats are usually quick.

After the last heat: parc fermé **≥ 30 minutes** for protests. Results stand **30 minutes** after posting. Protest window: **30 minutes** from the end of that race. No work, no tyre deflation, no removing the kart early. Mychron, transponder, and a cover are OK.

**Observation Form** (30 min from the incident) alerts officials; it is **not** a protest and they need not act. Formal protest is the GCR path.

## Strips

Protests or lap records can trigger a strip. Entrant present; mechanic/engine builder may strip with **your** tools under the TC/scrutineer. Nobody else. May be sealed and postponed.

Alcohol only after the meeting is officially over. Stay for prize-giving.
